## What's new

- The box works without the cable. Plug it in once and it pairs on its own. Then put it on Wi-Fi in Settings → Device, and VibeBuddy reaches it over your local network, or through the VibeBuddy Relay when you're elsewhere. The cable still wins when it's plugged in, and moving between the three doesn't drop the link.
- The Relay passes the box's messages through VibeBuddy's server. You can turn it off in Settings → General.
- A box links to one computer at a time. Another computer can take it over from Settings → Device.
- First-run setup now puts the box on Wi-Fi, and the Linux app gets a first-run guide too.
- Also: screenshots work over the network, the Linux Device tab scrolls, and joining Wi-Fi says whether it worked.
- Needs box firmware 0.5.0. Settings → Device offers it.
